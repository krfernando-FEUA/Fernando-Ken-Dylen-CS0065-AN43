# Character artwork

The Streamlit prototype bundles six transparent character illustrations derived from official Sanrio character pages. The files are used as small domain illustrations, not as a claim of endorsement. Character artwork belongs to Sanrio. Shops, prices and rules are fictional.

Images were cropped to transparent bounds, resized and centered in 512-pixel canvases in the earlier concept prototype. `ASSETS-ORIGINAL.md` retains the full original provenance record; its other icons/fonts belong to that earlier version and are not dependencies of this Streamlit app.
